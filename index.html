Palpease: Revolutionizing Breast Health Screening

Smart, Accessible, and Affordable Detection for All

🩺 The Problem

Breast cancer is the most common cancer in women worldwide, yet early detection remains a major challenge—especially in low-resource settings and among women who hesitate to undergo regular screening due to cost, discomfort, fear of radiation, or lack of access.

  • Mammography is expensive and inaccessible in rural or tier-2/3 areas.
  • Thermal-based solutions like Niramai require skilled operators and costly hardware.
  • Self-exams are unreliable due to human error or lack of technique.
  • Most solutions are either for professionals (B2B) or home users (B2C), rarely both.

✅ The Solution: Palpease

Palpease bridges the gap between accessibility and clinical-grade detection through a unique dual-technology platform tailored for both B2C (home users) and B2B (clinics, health workers).

🔹 B2C (Home Use)

  • Uses pressure sensing to mimic trained touch.
  • Non-invasive, radiation-free and comfortable for self-exams.
  • AI-powered instant feedback via mobile app.
  • Portable, affordable, and user-friendly.

🔹 B2B (Clinics/Professionals)

  • Combines pressure sensing with photoacoustic imaging.
  • Detects deeper tissue abnormalities with greater accuracy.
  • Ideal for health workers, mobile screening vans, rural clinics.
  • Real-time results improve early clinical intervention.

🔍 Why Palpease Stands Out

  • Dual-modality platform: tailored for both personal and professional use.
  • AI-powered insights for fast and accurate interpretation.
  • Radiation-free and painless detection for regular use.
  • Most affordable option compared to competitors.
