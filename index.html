Real-World Barriers to Early Detection

  • Tracking Changes is Difficult: Monthly checks are inconsistent, and memory of past sensations is unreliable.
  • Misinterpreting Symptoms: Benign and harmful lumps often feel alike, leading to uncertainty and stress.
  • Improper Techniques: Many know about Breast Self-Exams (BSE) but lack structured guidance or confidence.
  • Delayed Medical Attention: Anxiety, stigma, or confusion often prevent timely consultation, risking late diagnoses.

Innovative Tools

Users: Clinics & Health Professionals (B2B)

Technology: Pressure Sensing + Photoacoustic Imaging

Vedh offers an affordable, portable and scalable clinical screening alternative for early tumor detection. By integrating pressure sensing with deeper tissue insights, it delivers a powerful upgrade to conventional breast screening practices.
